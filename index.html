About the workshop

Make Physics tangible through experiments

This workshop focuses on innovative, low-cost experimental models that can support meaningful Physics teaching. Participants will engage with demonstrations, build and test models, and discuss how affordable experiments can be integrated into classroom practice.

🔬

Hands-on learning

Experience demonstrations and activities that make physical principles visible and easier to discuss.

🛠️

Model development

Build, test and refine affordable experimental models for teaching and learning.

💡

Share teaching ideas

Exchange practical strategies for bringing low-cost experiments into Physics classrooms.

Special feature

Innovative Physics Experiment Showcase

Share an original or creatively adapted Physics experiment that helps learners understand a concept. Participants can choose either of the following presentation formats.

Create · Demonstrate · Inspire

Show us a better way to teach Physics

Bring an idea that is scientifically sound, practical to reproduce and effective in explaining a Physics concept.

Format 1 · Working Model

Present a functioning experimental model and explain its scientific principle, setup, observations and classroom use.

Format 2 · Experiment Poster

Present a poster covering the principle, labelled apparatus/setup, procedure, observations, explanation and educational application.

Proposed award category
🏆 Best Low-Cost Innovative Physics Experiment
